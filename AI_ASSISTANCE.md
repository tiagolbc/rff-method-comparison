# AI-assisted development disclosure

OpenAI ChatGPT was used as an assistant during portions of the development and documentation of this project, including:

- drafting and debugging Python and MATLAB integration code;
- assisting with implementation and review of prespecified R analyses and validation procedures;
- improving documentation and English-language clarity.

The study design, method definitions, analytical outcomes, model specifications, inclusion and exclusion rules, and scientific interpretation were determined by the authors. All AI-assisted code and text were critically reviewed, executed, tested, corrected, and approved by the authors. Artificial intelligence was not used to generate or alter participant data or to make autonomous analytical or scientific decisions.
